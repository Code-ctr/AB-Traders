# abtraders

ABTrader is a business management application built with Flutter and Firebase that helps small and medium businesses manage their daily operations efficiently.

🔹 Key Features

Customer Management

Add and view customers with their details (name, address, phone, balance).

Track purchases, deposits, and outstanding balances.

Color-coded balance display (green = credit, red = debit).

Sales & Purchases

Record sales and purchase orders with itemized details.

Update customer balances automatically.

Store item details with quantity and price tracking.

Payments & Accounts

Accept payments via cash or bank.

Auto-update cash balance and bank balance.

Transaction history with date, amount, and payment method.

Reports & Analytics

Generate sales reports by customer, date, or store.

Track stock and profit ledgers.

Expense management with categorized entries.

Database Structure (Firebase Firestore)

Customer – Customer profiles and balances.

Item – Stock and product details.

Sale & Purchase – Transaction records.

Expense – Expense tracking.

Company/Investment & Bank – Finance management.

🛠 Tech Stack

Frontend: Flutter (Dart)

Backend/Database: Firebase Firestore

Authentication & Security: Firebase Auth
